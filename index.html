Common questions

The script doesn't follow my voice

Check that Microphone is allowed in Settings › Apps › Teleprompter, and that "Follow my voice" is on in Teleprompter's settings. The first time you use a language, your iPhone may need an internet connection to download Apple's speech model. If voice follow can't run, Teleprompter says why and scrolls at a steady speed instead.

Which languages does voice follow support?

Many, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese and Indonesian. It uses the speech recognition languages your iPhone supports, and Teleprompter detects your script's language automatically.

Where are my Film recordings?

In the Photos app. Teleprompter needs permission to add to Photos; you can check it in Settings › Apps › Teleprompter › Photos. If a take can't be saved, it isn't lost: Film keeps it and offers to try again or share it.

The floating window (Float) doesn't appear

Tap "Start floating" in Float. To have it start when you go to the Home Screen, turn on Settings › General › Picture in Picture › Start PiP Automatically. Some apps, like FaceTime, show their own floating window when you leave them, and only one can float at a time.

What do the [square brackets] do?

Anything in [square brackets] is a note to yourself, like [smile] or [pause]. It's shown in color, and voice follow doesn't wait for you to say it.

Can I use a remote or keyboard?

Yes. With a Bluetooth keyboard or presentation remote, Space starts and pauses, and the left and right arrow keys or the page keys move back and forward. At a steady speed, the up and down arrows change the speed.

Privacy

Teleprompter collects no data. Your scripts, voice and videos stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
