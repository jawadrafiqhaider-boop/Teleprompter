Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Teleprompter with Voice Follow (“Teleprompter”) is a teleprompter app for iPhone. It is built so that your scripts, your voice and your videos stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Teleprompter has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. The developer never receives your scripts, recordings, voice, or any information about how you use the app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Scripts and settings. The scripts you write or import and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, and in your iPhone's own backups if you use them.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. When voice follow is on, Teleprompter listens through the microphone and turns your speech into text with Apple's speech recognition, which runs on your iPhone, only to find your place in the script. That audio and text are used while the prompter is open and are not saved or sent anywhere. If the speech model for your language isn't on your iPhone yet, iOS downloads it from Apple. Your speech is not part of that download.
  • Camera. Used only in Film, to record you while you read.
  • Photos. Film saves your recordings to your photo library. Teleprompter asks only for permission to add to your library and cannot see your photos.
  • Picture in Picture. Float shows your script in a floating window. Teleprompter does not see or record anything from the other apps you use.

You can turn microphone, speech recognition, camera and Photos access on or off at any time in Settings › Teleprompter. Without them the related feature won't work, but the rest of the app will.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple and you control this, not the app.

Children

Teleprompter doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
